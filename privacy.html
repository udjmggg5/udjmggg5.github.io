Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

What ut-bot is

ut-bot is a private tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels. It is not offered to other users.

Data we access

When the owner signs in with Google, ut-bot uses YouTube API Services to access:

  • channel and video information (titles, descriptions, playlists, publishing status);
  • channel and video statistics from YouTube Analytics (views, watch time, retention and similar metrics);
  • permission to upload videos and update video metadata on the owner's channels.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to upload and schedule the owner's videos and to analyze the performance of the owner's channels. OAuth tokens and statistics are stored on a private server controlled by the owner. Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Third-party services

ut-bot uses YouTube API Services. By using ut-bot you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings. Stored data is deleted on request or when access is revoked, within 30 days.
